# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Give constructive feedback about work, respect privacy, and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing and deliberately disruptive behavior are prohibited in project spaces and when representing this community. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or ban participants proportionately to the behavior. Enforcement decisions must consider context and protect reporters' privacy.

Contact repository maintainers privately through their GitHub profiles to report concerns; do not publish private evidence in issues. If a maintainer is involved, contact another maintainer. A dedicated confidential reporting contact must be established before the v1 release.
