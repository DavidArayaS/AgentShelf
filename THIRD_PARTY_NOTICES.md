# Third-party notices

AgentShelf source is Apache-2.0. Dependencies retain their own licenses and copyright notices. Run `pnpm licenses:check` to generate `reports/licenses.json` for the exact installed transitive dependency tree, including development dependencies. Consult each dependency's distributed LICENSE and NOTICE files; this inventory does not replace those obligations.

Release artifacts must include the applicable dependency notices and SBOM. Do not publish while the dependency policy gate fails. No third-party protocol schemas are redistributed at this foundation stage.
